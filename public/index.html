How it works

I

Vetted retailers only

Prices come from a short, hand-picked list of established stores. No anonymous marketplace sellers, no lookalike storefronts.

II

Polite fetching

Each retailer is queried within a strict rate limit, results are cached, and robots.txt is respected. We would rather be a little stale than a nuisance.

III

Maths you can check

The simulator works the dice sequence step by step, hit, wound, save, damage, so every number can be traced back to a roll.

Check before you buy. Prices and stock change quickly and may be out of date or missing for some stores. Always confirm the final price, shipping and availability on the retailer's own site. Unit data may lag behind the latest rules updates.
