    Why trust these prices

    Stores & method
    I

    Vetted stores only

    A short, hand-picked list of established stockists. No marketplace sellers, no lookalike storefronts, and prices far below RRP are flagged instead of promoted.

    II

    Delivered, not sticker

    Every price includes estimated shipping and sales tax for the area we are set up for, so a cheap kit with expensive postage does not win.

    III

    Polite and honest

    Stores are checked within strict rate limits; the age of every price is shown. Estimates and sample data are always labelled.
